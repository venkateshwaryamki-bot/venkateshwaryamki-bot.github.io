    A Mahabharata Ludo experience

    Chakravyuh

    Four kingdoms race to the golden chakra at the heart of the field. Choose who rules Dwaraka, Hastinapur, Anga and Indraprastha. Each champion has a gift drawn from the epic, and every capture calls down an Astra.

    How to play

    The race (classic Ludo rules)

    • Each kingdom has 4 pieces. Roll a 6 to bring a piece out of the palace onto your conch square.
    • Move clockwise around the field, then up your coloured path to the chakra at the centre. You need the exact number to finish.
    • A 6, a capture, or bringing a piece home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row ends your turn.
    • Land on an opponent to capture it and send it back to its palace. Pieces on golden lotuses and conch squares are safe.
    • The first kingdom to bring all four pieces home wins.

    Astras

    • You draw an Astra when you capture or land on a chakra square.
    • You may play one Astra per turn, before you roll. You can hold 3.
