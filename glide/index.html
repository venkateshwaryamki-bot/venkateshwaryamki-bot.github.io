Glide Installation guide

Your phone is now your laptop's trackpad.

Turn your phone sideways and it works like a laptop trackpad, with click buttons, scrolling, a keyboard and media keys. Connect over Wi-Fi or a USB-C cable.

Download for Android How to set it up

Free · Android 7+ app · iPhone works in the browser · Windows, Mac and Linux

Set up in three steps

About 10 minutes the first time. After that, start Glide on the laptop and open the app.

  1. Start Glide on your laptop

    Press the Windows key, type PowerShell, press Enter. Paste this line (right-click) and press Enter:

    irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/venkateshwaryamki-bot/venkateshwaryamki-bot.github.io/main/glide/install.ps1 | iex

    It installs everything and puts a Glide icon on your desktop for next time.

    Download the ZIP, double-click it, open the glide folder, then right-click start.command → Open.

    Download ZIP

    Then allow Terminal in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility.

    Download the ZIP, extract it, and run ./start.sh in the glide folder. Needs an X11 session.

    Download ZIP

  2. Get Glide on your phone

    Android: download and open the app. Android will ask to allow installing it. Tap Allow, then Install.

    Download for Android

    iPhone: no app needed. Scan the QR code from step 3 with the camera, then use Share › Add to Home Screen.

  3. Connect

    Your laptop shows a QR code. In the Glide app, tap Scan QR code and point the camera at it. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

    Using a cable? Plug in a USB-C cable with USB debugging on and the app connects by itself. How to turn it on

What you get

Everything a laptop trackpad does, plus a few extras.

Laptop-style trackpadClick buttons along the bottom edge and a scroll zone on the right. Switch between horizontal and vertical any time.
Real trackpad gesturesTap to click, two-finger scroll with momentum, pinch to zoom, hold to drag, three-finger swipes.
Keyboard and media keysType on your phone, use arrow keys and shortcuts, and control music and volume.
Wi-Fi or USB-C cableFinds your laptop by itself on Wi-Fi, or connects over a cable with about 1 ms delay.

Private by design. Glide only talks to devices on your own network or cable. Nothing goes to the internet. Only scan the QR code from your own laptop, and avoid typing passwords through Glide on public Wi-Fi.
