Chronicle

    Astra Ludo

    Long before Ludo, India raced tokens around a cross-shaped cloth in Pachisi and Chaupar, and legend ties it to the fateful game of dice in the Mahabharata. Here the four Dikpalas, guardians of the directions, race their tokens home. Each one carries a divine gift, and every capture calls down an Astra.

    How to play

    The race

    • Each Guardian has 4 tokens. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your yard onto your start square.
    • Move clockwise around the track, then up your coloured home path into the centre. You need the exact number to finish.
    • A 6, a capture, or bringing a token home earns another roll. Three 6s in a row and the gods end your turn.
    • Land on an opponent to capture it and send it back to its yard. Tokens on stars and start squares are safe.
    • The first Guardian to bring all four tokens home wins.

    Astras: divine power cards

    • You draw an Astra when you capture or land on a glowing Mandala square.
    • You may play one Astra per turn, before you roll. You can hold 3 (Kubera can hold 4).
